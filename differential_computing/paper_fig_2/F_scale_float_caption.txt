CAPTION (draft):
The strategy selector and its compiler price. LEFT (table): structure — not size — chooses PSR
vs NSR. Aligned tangents (χ→1) favour PSR by a landscape factor (co-located ± branches are
common-mode-free); compressible tangents (χ<1) favour NSR by χ⁻² (one folded shift where PSR
pays per term); a non-involutive generator admits no clean ±π/2 shift, so only NSR applies.
COMPRESSION IS A FAILURE OF JOINT EXTREMIZABILITY of the weighted tangent Σ_j v_j H_j — its joint
value cannot reach Σ_j|v_j|·diam(H_j) — reachable by EITHER shared-support cancellation in a
COMMUTING family (telescoping, χ=O(1/m)) OR non-commuting contraction (Heisenberg, χ≈0.5); it does
NOT require non-commutativity. Compile overhead is per-strategy (footer): NSR adds only
coefficient arrays (+0 segments; the lowering is the identity on segments), PSR adds +k inserted
segments (motion and one gate). RIGHT (strip): the INCREMENTAL cost of differentiation over the
source compilation path, log-log, three series (source | +PSR/branch | +NSR/branch). The
differentiation increment is a per-branch MAPPING pass over the machine-native segments; the
evolution SOLVE is shared across branches, so "per branch" is the MARGINAL cost (S4-i): at n=1000
it is 0.3% of source (the earlier 0.4% quoted a full recompile — the strip and this caption use
the marginal number). Total derivative-compile cost = per-branch × 2m × P; at F6/F-loop's m it is
reported in the note. NSR's increment is ~10³× smaller still (coefficient-only). TIMING stops at
the machine-native SEGMENTS (S4-ii); the pulse/scheduler layer is out of scope for these numbers.
SCOPE (S3): the generic all-pairs solver caps at n=12; every point beyond runs on the
target-aware SPECIALIZED 1D-chain path (frozen geometry, bond-pruned lines, analytic warm start,
sparse Jacobian) — the n=1000 reach is on that path, stated wherever the number appears; the
generic ceiling is a solver story, kept in the appendix. Dispersion: IQR bands over the repeated
timings (median where compile < 5 s); machine/warm-up/cache policy and the two-regime PSR slope
(≈1 below n≈200, ≈2 above; the strip's marginal per-branch is ≈1.5) are in the appendix F-scale
figure. Compiled Hamiltonian matches the target to machine precision (max |dH| ≤ 5e-13); the 1D
truncated-dressing tail is ~1.5% relative, declared. Honesty rail: source compilation dominates at
large n — differentiation is not the bottleneck.
