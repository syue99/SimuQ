Closing the optimization loop on a deliberately ill-conditioned valley: median ± IQR over 20 seeds of ‖θ_t−θ*‖, 50 gradient-descent steps. 2q TFIM (P=2), compiled to machine-native segments, emulated under T4 at T/T2*=0.15. Minimised objective: C(θ)=(⟨Z0Z1⟩−a*)²+w(⟨Z0+Z1⟩−b*)² with a*=+0.597, b*=-1.331 the exact values at the interior θ*=[1.0, 1.0] and w=0.25 — a residual form whose minimum is θ* by construction (no boundary clipping, no prior term). The small residual weight makes the valley SLOPPY (soft curvature μ_soft=0.54, κ~1/w) on purpose: an ill-conditioned valley is where a biased gradient is most likely to go wrong, hence the informative test case. Every method spends the same 6000-shot budget per gradient, so a step costs the same for all series and the step axis is directly comparable. FD is the honest black-box central difference of the noisy objective at three ε: too large fails outright (truncation (ε²/6)|C‴|/μ_soft), too small is unreliable (δ/ε jitter, wide IQR), and the retrospectively best ε (unknowable without θ*) floors just above tolerance. Figure (F-loop-full, appendix two-panel variant). The loop closes for the ε-free strategies; finite differences settle short. Honest IQS recovery of an INTERIOR, centered θ*=[1.0, 1.0] for a 2-parameter TFIM instance (P=2). Objective C=(⟨Z0Z1⟩−a*)²+w(⟨Z0+Z1⟩−b*)² (two Z-diagonal observables, one readout; a*,b* the exact values at θ*, so θ* is C's min by construction — the single-observable ⟨O⟩+prior form has no interior stationary point and clips to an edge). w=0.25 sets a SLOPPY valley (soft-direction curvature μ_soft=0.54, κ~1/w). Compiled to machine-native segments, emulated under T4 at T/T2*=0.15; real estimators, no surrogates; identical 6000-shot budget per gradient (step axis directly comparable). PSR/NSR assemble the ANALYTIC gradient 2·r_a·∇⟨O_a⟩+2w·r_b·∇⟨O_b⟩ from ε-free observable-gradient estimators (residuals measured at θ) ⇒ unbiased and ε-FREE ⇒ they crawl the valley to θ* and hold within tolerance, no step size to choose. FD is the honest BLACK-BOX central difference of the noisy objective C, and it needs an ε it cannot set without already knowing θ*: the too-large ε FAILS outright (truncation (ε²/6)|C‴| does not self-correct — C‴(θ*)≠0 — and divides by the small μ_soft into a large offset); the too-small ε is UNRELIABLE (δ/ε amplifies the resolution jitter into a wide-IQR random walk); and even the retrospectively best ε (which requires θ* to pick) only reaches an UNCERTIFIABLE δ-floor a hair above tolerance. HONEST NOTE: unlike the gradient-space floor (F6), the δ/ε variance would vanish exactly at a flat optimum, so a perfectly tuned small ε can limp in — the point is not that every ε lands far, it is that FD carries an ε-tuning burden (unknowable a-priori, failing on either side) that the ε-free strategies simply do not have. No injected calibration offset — the trap is the sloppy conditioning (κ~1/μ_soft), the paper's thesis. Tolerance 0.03≈1.5δ. Predicted truncation b(ε)/μ_soft reported beside the measured offset below; we report disagreement, we do not scale to fit. Scope: FD is unreliable, not always worse — a biased estimator can point better at a particular operating point (so can a random direction); the sound strategies converge without that luck.
