Compile-time scaling for the 1D TFIM chain on the rydberg2d AAIS (differentiation-first pipeline). Left: the generic all-pairs machine grows with fitted slope ~4.4 and is practically capped at n=12 (27 s); the target-aware specialization layer (frozen chain geometry, bond-pruned ZZ/dressing lines, analytic warm start, sparse Jacobian) compiles the SAME AAIS with slope ~1.9, reaching n=1000 in 59 s (up to 752x at overlapping sizes); the warm start is a residual-zero point, so the solve verifies rather than searches. Right: differentiation adds only a per-branch mapping pass (evolution solve shared across branches): 242 ms/branch at n=1000 (slope ~1.5). Compiled Hamiltonian matches the target to machine precision at every size (max |dH| <= 5e-13); the truncated dressing tail is declared in the plan (dropped_zz field, ~1.5% relative). Data: compile_scaling_native.json (median of repeated timings where compile < 5 s).
