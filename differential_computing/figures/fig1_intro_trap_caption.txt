Figure 1. The finite-difference trap. Large steps ε (orange) mis-sign the gradient — the secant straddles a feature. But ε cannot be shrunk freely: it is bounded below by the control-setpoint resolution δ, and near that floor δ (amplified by 1/ε) scatters the estimate into a wide noise cone (purple, Sec. 6.2). Either sound strategy (PSR/NSR, blue) recovers the noisy slope with no step-size to tune.
