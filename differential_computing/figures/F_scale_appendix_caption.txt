F-scale appendix. (a) Source compilation to machine-native schedule ops + pulse ledger: the generic all-pairs path (slope ~4.4) is practically capped at n=12; the target-aware specialized path (this claim is scoped to 1D chains and full 2D NN grids) reaches n=1000 (1D, slope ~1.9, 60 s) and 32x32=1024 (2D, slope ~2.0); IQR bands over 3 compiles. (b) Marginal cost per derivative branch. PSR is two-regime: slope ~1.1 below n~200 and ~1.5 above; the steepening is driven by the ledger position-snapshot pass (n positions x O(n) plays, structurally O(n^2) — its share grows from ~20% to ~36%), on top of the near-linear dressing-H rebuild, the largest single pass at n=1000 (inset: measured per-pass shares). NSR needs only an O(n) coefficient table on the shared schedule. Totals for one full gradient at n=1000 (m=48 tau-samples, G2): PSR 2mM = 95,904 branches -> 4.7 h of mapping (amortizable: branches share segment structure, tau enters only as two durations); NSR 2N = 16 branches -> 3.1 ms total.
